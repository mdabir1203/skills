# Brief Template

The 8 questions that lock the brief. Ask them in order. Do not paraphrase — ask exactly as written. The first three questions are the most important; the last five fill in the texture.

## Required

### 1. Product / topic
What is the thing? One noun phrase. If the user says "a SaaS platform for X", push for the product name. If the product does not have a name yet, the video is not ready.

### 2. Audience
Who is watching, and what do they currently believe about this category? "Everyone" is not an answer. Push for a specific person — their job title, their morning, the Slack channel they live in. The belief they currently hold is more important than the demographics.

### 3. Single outcome
What should the viewer DO or FEEL after 30 seconds? One sentence, present tense. "They should sign up" is a feature. "They should feel like they have been using the wrong tool for two years" is an outcome. The second is a video; the first is a landing page.

### 4. Length
15s, 30s, 60s, 90s, 120s. Default: 30s for product explainers, 60–90s for launches, 120s only for true product films. If the user asks for "a 2-minute explainer", ask what they would cut to make it 60 seconds. The cuts they refuse to make tell you the actual single outcome.

### 5. Frame
- **16:9** (1920×1080) — YouTube, web hero, embedded
- **9:16** (1080×1920) — Reels, TikTok, Shorts
- **1:1** (1080×1080) — social square, LinkedIn
- **4:5** (1080×1350) — IG feed
- **Custom** — only with a real reason

For 9:16, every horizontal layout needs to be re-staged. Do not just crop a 16:9. The squint test on a cropped 9:16 is brutal.

### 6. Reference world
- **Apple** — single subject, slow, premium, emotional score
- **Google I/O** — modular, optimistic, bright, friendly
- **Silicon Valley editorial** — dark, glass, gradient, mono accents
- **Other** — describe with 2–3 reference links

### 7. Tone
One word from this set: **heroic, intimate, urgent, playful, mysterious, calm, defiant, melancholic**. If the user picks two, they have to pick one. Two tones is a confused video.

### 8. Hard constraints
- Brand guidelines (logo lockup, color codes, font, dos and don'ts)
- Must-include elements (specific feature, specific quote, specific stat)
- Music licensing (own it, licensed, royalty-free, AI-generated)
- Talent releases (if any human appears on screen)
- Legal copy (FTC disclosures, "results not typical", etc.)
- Forbidden elements (no competitors shown, no certain imagery, etc.)
- Delivery deadline + review cycle

## Optional but useful

- **One competitor you admire** — tells you the taste ceiling
- **One video you wish you had made** — tells you the actual reference
- **One video you hated** — tells you the failure mode to avoid
- **Where will this video live?** (homepage hero, paid social, sales deck, conference opener) — context changes the cut
- **Is there an existing brand kit?** (Figma library, Notion page, Slack channel) — pull from it before generating

## When the brief is ready

Move to Stage 2 — Concept. The concept must restate the brief in 3–4 sentences using the hook + arc + closer structure from the main SKILL.md. If the concept does not fit, the brief is too long.
