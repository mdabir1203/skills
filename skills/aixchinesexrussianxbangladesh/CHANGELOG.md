## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-02

### Added
- Initial release of the `aixchinesexrussianxbangladesh` skill
- 17 principles organized as a behavioral guardrail, not a procedural workflow
- Business Reality (principle 1) — six domain-specific workflow verifications (Money, Production, Automation, AI, Security, Data migration)
- Context-Adaptive Verification (principle 2) — adapt the checklist to the domain
- Proportional Rigor (principle 3) — risk × complexity × business impact
- No Fake Verification (principle 5) — explicit VERIFIED / INFERRED / NOT VERIFIED / BLOCKED / PRE-EXISTING markers
- 18-item Completion Gate (principle 14)
- Final Completion Test (principle 15) — would a skeptical engineer, a real user, and the business owner all agree?
- The Judge (principle 17) — judged by verified outcomes, not code volume
- No `references/` or `assets/` because this is a philosophy, not a procedural workflow
